Privacy Policy

App: MySpendbook (com.myspendbook.app) · Developer: Budde Bharath Kumar · Effective date: 10 November 2026

MySpendbook ("the app") is a personal expense tracker for users in India, developed and published on Google Play by Budde Bharath Kumar ("we", "our"). This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and the choices you have. MySpendbook was earlier called "DailySpendbook"; this policy replaces the earlier version.

1. What the app does and why it reads bank SMS

MySpendbook brings the spending from all your bank accounts, cards and UPI apps into one place: daily and date-range totals; filters by payment app, bank, category, person or shop, and the last 4 digits of a card or account; analytics and comparisons; and tracking of credit card bills, EMIs, insurance premiums and subscription renewals with reminders before they are due.

Payment apps do not send a notification for every payment, and many payments (card swipes, auto-debits, EMIs) have no payment-app notification at all. Your bank SMS is the complete record, so reading it is the core feature of the app.

2. Information we collect

a) Account details

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address. We use them only to create and identify your account.

b) Bank SMS (only if you allow SMS access)

With your permission, the app reads SMS on your phone to find messages from banks, card companies and lenders about debits, card spends, card bills, EMIs, due dates and subscription renewals. The message is read on your phone; OTPs, personal messages, promotions and all other SMS are ignored and are not stored or uploaded.

From a transaction message we save only the transaction details: amount, date and time, payee or merchant name, bank or card name with the last digits of the card or account, the bank's reference number, transaction type and a category. You can also ask the app to read the SMS of dates you choose again ("Refetch records") or of the last 30 days (Settings → "Re-import last 30 days of SMS").

c) Payment notifications (only if you allow Notification access)

With your permission, the app checks notifications from payment apps (for example PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM), bank apps, and – only when the sender is a bank, card company or lender – WhatsApp and SMS apps, to catch payments and bill reminders. Notifications that are not about a payment, bill or due date (including personal and group chats) are ignored on your phone and are not stored. The app does not read email.

d) Original message text

The full original text of an SMS or notification is read on your phone and is never sent to our servers.

e) Things you add yourself

Manual entries, categories, the names you mark as Transfer, and the bills and subscriptions you track.

f) Data kept only on your phone

Your app lock PIN (stored as a salted hash), the fingerprint setting, your language and your daily-card settings stay on your phone. Fingerprint checks are done by Android; we never receive fingerprint data.

g) Advertising data (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob (a banner and occasional full-screen ads). Google may collect your device's Advertising ID, IP address (which gives an approximate location), and basic device, app-interaction and diagnostic information to show and measure ads. We never share your transactions, messages or financial data with Google AdMob or any advertiser. Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Premium subscribers see no ads.

h) Purchases

Premium subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We store only your plan, its expiry date, whether it renews, its status (for example, a renewal payment problem) and Google's purchase reference, so Premium can be switched on for your account and you can be told when it ends. We never see your card, UPI or bank payment details.

We do not collect your contacts, precise location, call logs, photos, or any health data. The app never sends SMS and never changes your SMS inbox. MySpendbook does not give loans, credit scores or credit offers, and never uses your SMS or payment data for those purposes.

3. How we use your information

  • To show your spending, categories, filters, summaries, charts and reports.
  • To track your bills (total due, paid and pending) and remind you at 9:30 AM the day before and on the due date while something is unpaid.
  • To send daily cards (up to 3 a day: a short thought, a saving tip, and a summary of yesterday's spending with how much you could have saved). Amounts are shown only inside the app, never in a notification.
  • To keep your account secure and to sync your data when you sign in.
  • To show ads in the free version.

We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not use your financial data for advertising.

6. Storage and security

Your account and records are stored on Supabase cloud servers in the Asia-Pacific (Singapore) region and are encrypted at rest. All data between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS), and access rules make sure only your account can read your data.

7. How long we keep data and how to delete it

Transactions and bill records older than 12 months are deleted automatically. You can delete your account and all data at any time in the app: Settings → "Delete account and all data". This permanently removes your account, transactions, bills and subscriptions from our database right away. Any copies in routine server backups are removed within 30 days. You can also request deletion by email – see Delete Account.

8. Your rights

You can view and correct your data in the app, download it ("Download Transactions"), and delete it at any time. You can also withdraw any permission at any time in Settings or Android Settings.

Grievance contact: Budde Bharath Kumar, dailypay.dp@gmail.com. We reply to every request or complaint within 30 days.

9. Children

MySpendbook is meant for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
