Privacy Policy

App: MySpendbook (com.myspendbook.app) · Developer: Budde Bharath Kumar · Effective date: 10 November 2026

MySpendbook ("the app") is a personal expense tracker for users in India, developed and published on Google Play by Budde Bharath Kumar ("we", "our"). This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and the choices you have. MySpendbook was earlier called "DailySpendbook"; this policy replaces the earlier version.

1. Information we collect

a) Account details. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address. We use them only to create and identify your account.

b) Bank SMS (only if you allow SMS access). Reading bank SMS is the core feature of MySpendbook. With your permission, the app reads SMS on your phone to find messages from banks and payment services about debits, credits, card bills, EMIs, due dates and subscription renewals. Personal messages and OTPs are ignored and are not stored.

From a transaction message we save only the transaction details: amount, date and time, payee or merchant name, bank or card name with the last 4 digits, transaction type and a category. You can also ask the app to re-read the last 30 days of SMS (Settings → "Re-import last 30 days of SMS").

c) Payment notifications (only if you allow Notification access). With your permission, the app checks notifications from payment, banking and messaging apps (for example PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, bank apps, WhatsApp, Gmail) to catch payments and bill reminders that do not come as SMS. Notifications that are not about a payment, bill or due date are ignored on your phone and are not stored.

d) Original message text (off by default). The full original text of a message is not saved. Only if you switch on "Save original messages (testing)" in Settings, the exact text is kept with each payment in your own account so mistakes can be checked. This switch turns itself off after 7 days, and "Erase saved original messages" deletes all of them at any time.

e) Things you add yourself. Manual entries, categories, notes, and the bills and subscriptions you track.

f) Data kept only on your phone. Your app lock PIN, the fingerprint setting, your card language and your daily-card settings stay on your phone. Fingerprint checks are done by Android; we never receive fingerprint data.

g) Advertising data (Google AdMob). The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's Advertising ID, IP address and basic device information to show and measure ads. We never share your transactions, messages or financial data with Google AdMob or any advertiser. Learn more: How Google uses information in ads. Premium subscribers see no ads.

h) Purchases. Premium subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We receive only the purchase status (active or not). We never see your card, UPI or bank payment details.

We do not collect your contacts, location, call logs, photos, or any health data. MySpendbook does not give loans, credit scores or credit offers, and never uses your SMS or payment data for those purposes.

2. How we use your information

  • To show your spending, categories, summaries, charts and reports.
  • To remind you before a credit card bill, EMI or subscription renewal is due.
  • To send daily cards (up to 3 a day: a short thought, a saving tip, and yesterday's spending summary). You can pause or turn them off in Settings.
  • To keep your account secure and to sync your data when you sign in.
  • To show ads in the free version.

We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not use your financial data for advertising.

5. Storage and security

Your account and records are stored on Supabase cloud servers located in Mumbai, India, and are encrypted at rest. All data between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS), and access rules make sure only your account can read your data.

6. How long we keep data and how to delete it

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account and all data at any time in the app: More → Settings → "Delete account and all data". This permanently removes your account, transactions, bills and subscriptions from our database right away. Copies in routine server backups are removed within 30 days. You can also request deletion by email — see Delete Account.

7. Your rights

You can view and correct your data in the app, download it ("Download Transactions"), and delete it at any time. You can also withdraw any permission at any time in Settings or Android Settings.

Grievance contact: Budde Bharath Kumar, dailypay.dp@gmail.com. We reply to every request or complaint within 30 days.

8. Children

MySpendbook is meant for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
