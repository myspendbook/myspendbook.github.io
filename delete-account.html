Delete Your MySpendbook Account

App: MySpendbook (com.myspendbook.app) on Google Play · Developer: Budde Bharath Kumar · Contact: dailypay.dp@gmail.com

Option 1 – Inside the app (instant)

  1. Open MySpendbook.
  2. Go to More → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and all data.
  4. Tap Delete permanently to confirm.

Your account and all data are deleted from our servers at once.

Option 2 – By email (if you no longer have the app)

Send an email to dailypay.dp@gmail.com from the Google account you used in MySpendbook, with the subject "Delete my MySpendbook account". We will delete your account and data within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account (name and email)
  • All transactions, categories and notes
  • Tracked bills, EMIs and subscriptions
  • Any saved original message text

What is kept

Nothing is kept in our database. Copies in routine server backups are removed automatically within 30 days. Data stored only on your phone (app lock PIN, card settings) is removed when you uninstall the app.

Premium subscription: deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
