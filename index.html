MySpendbook

Personal expense tracker for India · Android app by Budde Bharath Kumar

MySpendbook records your spending automatically from bank SMS and payment notifications, so you don't have to type every expense. See where your money goes each day and month, get a polite reminder before a credit card bill, EMI or subscription is due, and enjoy a short daily money check-in in 12 Indian languages.
