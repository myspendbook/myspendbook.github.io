1. The service

MySpendbook helps you track your spending in India. With your permission it records transactions from your bank SMS and payment notifications, and you can add entries yourself. It brings all your bank accounts, cards and UPI apps into one list; shows totals for a day or any date range; lets you filter by payment app, bank, category, person or shop, and the last 4 digits of a card or account; shows analytics and comparisons; tracks credit card bills, EMIs, insurance premiums and subscription renewals (total due, paid and pending); and reminds you before they are due. The app is meant for users in India.

2. How totals are counted

  • Card purchases are counted on the day you spend. Paying the card bill later is shown, but it is not added to your spending again.
  • ATM withdrawals, bank messages that say "self transfer", and payments to people you have marked as Transfer are not counted as spending.
  • A payment to your own name is not always recognised as a self transfer automatically; mark that name as Transfer once and the app remembers it.

3. Accuracy

The app reads messages automatically. Messages can be late, missing or worded differently, so an entry can sometimes be missed, duplicated or put in the wrong category. Please check important amounts against your bank statement. You can edit or delete any entry in the app.

5. Payment reminders

  • Reminders come at 9:30 AM the day before and on the due date, only while something is still unpaid.
  • If you allow full-screen alerts, the reminder opens as a full-screen card, also on the lock screen. Otherwise it comes as a notification. Android battery saving, "force stop" or turned-off permissions can delay or stop reminders.
  • Reminders are a help, not a guarantee. Always check the due date and amount with your bank. We are not responsible for late fees or charges.

9. Daily cards and the morning spend summary

  • The app can send up to 3 short cards a day: around 8:45 AM, 4 PM and 8 PM, in the language you choose.
  • The morning card shows a summary of your yesterday's non-mandatory ("fun") spending, worked out from your own records in the app, and how much you could have kept by saving at least 30% of it (the same "Potential Saving" shown in the app). Bills, EMIs, insurance, medical and education payments are treated as mandatory and are not counted.
  • This summary is an estimate to encourage saving, not financial advice. It is only as accurate as the transactions recorded in the app.
  • Amounts are shown only inside the app, never in the notification on your lock screen.
  • You can pause cards for 7 days, turn off a single card, or turn all cards off in Settings.
  • Thoughts and proverbs are from traditional and public-domain sources or written by us. The quote part of a card can be shared as an image; your spending summary is never included in a shared image.
